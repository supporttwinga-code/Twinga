1. Who we are

This policy explains how ("Twinga", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our apps: Twinga (for customers), Twinga Partner (for restaurants) and Twinga Rider (for delivery partners). Twinga connects customers, restaurants and delivery partners so that food can be ordered and delivered.

By using an app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

All users

  • Your name.
  • Your email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it), or your mobile number when you log in with an OTP.
  • Your role (customer, restaurant or delivery partner) and the date you joined.
  • A notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so that we can send you notifications.

Customers

  • Delivery addresses you save: label, address line, landmark and map position (latitude and longitude).
  • Your device location, only when you tap "Use my current location" or use location features. It is used to fill your address and to show restaurants that deliver to you.
  • Your orders: items, amounts, coupon used, delivery status, and any rating or review you give.

Restaurants

  • Restaurant name, cuisines, phone number, address, map position, delivery radius, packaging charge and preparation time.
  • Your FSSAI licence number and the photos or logo you upload.
  • Your menu, prices, availability and the orders you receive.
  • Payment details for withdrawals: UPI ID or bank account holder name, account number and IFSC code.

Delivery partners

  • Name, phone number, city, vehicle type and vehicle number, and the photo you upload.
  • Your live location while you are online and the app is open, so that we can assign nearby orders and customers can see where their order is.
  • Deliveries you complete, your earnings, cash collected and cash deposited.
  • Payment details for withdrawals: UPI ID or bank account details.

We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos or files other than what you choose to upload in the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and to log you in.
  • To show restaurants near you, take your order, and deliver it.
  • To calculate bills, delivery charges, commission, earnings and payouts.
  • To send notifications about your orders and, if you allow, offers.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, to solve complaints, and to meet legal and tax duties.
  • To improve the apps and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising in our apps.

4. Who we share it with

  • Restaurant: your name and the items you ordered. The delivery address is used by the delivery partner.
  • Delivery partner: your name, delivery address and map position so the order can reach you.
  • Customer: the delivery partner's name, phone number and live location during an active order, and the restaurant's name and address.
  • Twinga admin team: all information above, to run the service, handle support and pay restaurants and delivery partners.
  • Authorities: if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

6. Location

We ask for location permission only to fill your address, find restaurants and rider matching, and to show live delivery tracking. The delivery partner app sends the rider's location only while the rider is online and the app is open. You can turn the permission off in your phone settings, but some features will then not work.

7. Notifications

We send order updates and sometimes offers. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time. When you log out, the app stops receiving group notifications.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is active. Order, payment and earnings records are kept for as long as the law and accounting rules require, even after you delete your account. After that they are deleted or made anonymous.

9. Security

We use Google Firebase security rules, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and a secure server so that people can only see the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your phone and password private.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information.
  • You can withdraw location and notification permission in your phone settings.
  • To delete your account, see the Delete account page.

11. Children

Our apps are meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app.
