Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel an order in the app before the restaurant accepts it. You are not charged.
  • Once the restaurant has accepted or started preparing your food, the order usually cannot be cancelled, because the food is already being made.
  • If the restaurant rejects your order, or Twinga has to cancel it (for example no delivery partner is available), you do not pay for it.

2. When you can get a refund

  • The restaurant rejects or cancels your order after you paid online.
  • Your order is not delivered.
  • Items are missing or the order is wrong. Please tell us the same day with the order number, and a photo if possible.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

We look into every complaint and decide a fair solution. This may be a full or partial refund.

3. How refunds are paid

  • Online payments (UPI, cards, netbanking) are refunded to the same payment method you used. After we approve a refund it usually reaches you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Cash on delivery orders: you do not pay if the order is not delivered. If a refund is approved after you paid cash, we return the amount to your UPI or bank account.

4. When a refund may not be given

  • You gave a wrong address or were not reachable at the delivery address.
  • You changed your mind after the food was accepted or prepared.
  • The complaint is about taste or personal preference and the food was as described.

5. How to ask for a refund

Open the app, go to Profile, Help with an order and write to us, or email us with your order number.
