What is Twinga?

Twinga is a food delivery service. You choose a restaurant near you, place your order in the app, and a delivery partner brings it to your door. Restaurants prepare the food. Twinga connects customers, restaurants and delivery partners.

How it works

1. ChooseOpen the Twinga app, set your delivery address and pick from restaurants that deliver to you.
2. Order and paySee the full bill before you order: food, packaging, delivery, platform fee and taxes. Pay cash on delivery, or online where available.
3. Track and receiveFollow your order live. Share the delivery OTP with the delivery partner when the food is in your hands.

For restaurants

Own a restaurant? Use the Twinga Partner app to register your restaurant, add your menu with photos, set your delivery area and receive orders. Payouts are sent to your bank account or UPI.

For delivery partners

Want to earn by delivering? Use the Twinga Rider app to sign up, go online, accept nearby deliveries with in-app navigation, and withdraw your earnings.
