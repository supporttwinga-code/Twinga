1. What we deliver

Twinga delivers ready-to-eat food from restaurants that are listed in the Twinga app. We do not ship products by courier or post.

2. Where we deliver

Each restaurant sets its own delivery area. You can order only from restaurants that deliver to your saved address. If a restaurant does not deliver to you, it is not shown in the app.

3. Delivery time

Delivery time depends on how long the restaurant takes to prepare the food, the distance, traffic and weather. The app shows the order status and live tracking after a delivery partner picks up your order. Times shown are estimates, not a guarantee.

4. Delivery charges

The delivery charge, packaging charge, platform fee and taxes are shown on the bill before you place the order. You pay the amount on that bill.

5. Receiving your order

  • Please be available at your address and keep your phone on.
  • You get a delivery OTP in the app. Give it to the delivery partner only when the food is in your hands.
  • If we cannot reach you or you are not at the address, the order may be closed without a refund.

6. Problems with delivery

If your order is late, wrong or not delivered, contact us the same day. See our Cancellation and Refund Policy.
